Lepanto and Catholic memory

The Battle of Lepanto belongs first to history. Catholic devotion remembers it alongside Rosary prayer, thanksgiving, and the feast of Our Lady of the Rosary.

The exhibit presents the event without triumphalism: prayer is shown as dependence on God, not as a tool for spectacle.
